Beyond the words.

Tone Track turns a recording into a timeline you can listen to, read, and explore.

01

Bring your audio

Upload a file or record a short clip. The service transcribes the speech and analyzes overlapping audio windows.

02

Explore three dimensions

Valence estimates positivity, arousal estimates energy, and dominance estimates assertiveness. Higher values mean more of that dimension, not better performance.

03

Listen with context

Scores are model estimates, not facts about someone’s feelings. Accents, background noise, and context affect the results. AI notes can be wrong; the audio is your reference.
