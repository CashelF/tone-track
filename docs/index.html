About Tone Track

Upload audio or record your voice to get a transcript and a timeline of three estimates:

Valence
Positive or negative tone.
Arousal
Energy in the voice.
Dominance
Assertiveness of the delivery.

Scores run from 0 to 1. The numbers beside the timeline legend are averages. Click the chart or transcript to seek; click a legend label to hide a line.

These are model estimates, not facts about someone’s feelings. Context, accents, and background noise affect the results. AI notes appear when available.

Sample: John F. Kennedy’s 1961 inaugural address, public domain.
